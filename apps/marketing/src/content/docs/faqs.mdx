<Accordion title="What should I do if I encounter a bug?">
  You may report it in our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/rosace) or by contacting
  [us](mailto:team@rosace.app).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is Rosace different to Cavalry, Jitter, and After Effects?">
  After Effects is a powerful tool for creating animations, but it's also complex and expensive.

Cavalry is nonetheless free, but as complex as After Effects.

Jitter is a great tool for creating animations, but it's also complex and expensive.

Rosace is a free tool for creating animations. It's easy to use and doesn't require any design experience.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you add watermarks to exports?">No. Not on any plan.</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What formats can I export to?">
  MP4, WEBM, and more. All ratios — 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 — one click away.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you collect any data?">
  We respect [privacy](/privacy-policy) and never collect any sensitive data. Some interactions in
  the app are tracked anonymously. For instance, we save usage statistics and error reports to
  constantly fix bugs and improve our product.
</Accordion>
