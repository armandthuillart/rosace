- Ensuring the proper functioning of the site and improving the browsing experience.

The data collected in the context of implementing each of the purposes described above are limited to what is strictly necessary.

## Categories of data processed and retention period

The categories of data that may be processed depending on the purpose pursued are as follows:

- Data relating to site audience measurement.
- Data relating to person identification.
- Data relating to web browsing (timestamp, user IP address, etc.).

This website undertakes to ensure that your data is retained for a period that does not exceed what is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.

- Data necessary for account creation and management and data stored in personal storage space are retained as long as the account is active.
- Log data is retained for 30 days.
- Data necessary for producing audience and online service usage statistics is retained for 13 months in a format that does not allow identification of persons by their IP address, and includes an identifier (related to the cookie) retained for a maximum period of 7 days unless the person concerned objects.

You can exercise your rights or obtain information about the processing of your personal data directly from the data controller by email at the following address: [policies@rosace.app](mailto:policies@rosace.app).

## Use of connection cookies ("cookies")

This website uses connection cookies (called "cookies") that are placed on the user's computer, mobile or tablet. The complete list of third parties that place or read cookies on your device is available on the "cookie management" page accessible at the bottom of the site.

<aside>

A "cookie" is a sequence of information, generally small in size and identified by a name, that can be transmitted to your browser by a website to which the internet user connects. The internet user's web browser will keep it for a certain period, and will send it back to the web server each time the internet user reconnects to it.

</aside>

## How to delete cookies?

### Cookie management

You can configure your browser to notify you of the presence of cookies and offer you to accept or refuse them.

You can accept or refuse cookies on a case-by-case basis or refuse them once and for all.

It should be noted that this setting may modify your access conditions to site services requiring the use of cookies.

Cookie settings are different for each browser and are generally described in the help menus. You can disable cookies by following your browser's instructions:

- Edge: Consult the [Edge help page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09).
- Firefox: Consult the [Firefox help page](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox).
- Safari: Consult the [Safari help page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082).
- Google Chrome: Consult the [Chrome help page](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en).
- Opera: Consult the [Opera help page](https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/#cookies).
